06Questions

Good to know.

What are agent credits?

A dollar balance for AI models. Credits sit in an account as USDC, and one key from that account works across many models, from chat to image generation. You spend them as you use the models.

Do I need an account first?

No. beaconAgent gives your wallet an account the first time it is needed: when someone sends you credits, or once you have backed about $1 of a token. You open it by signing a message with your wallet. No password, no transaction.

Can I take my earnings as ETH instead?

No. Backers are paid in compute credits. That is the point: the fees a token earns become compute for the people backing it. Builders are the ones paid in ETH.

How often are backers paid?

Fees reach backers as a steady stream over 24 hours. Once you have earned about $2 in total, it is collected (checked every hour), confirmed, and sent to your account as credits. Small amounts simply wait until they add up.

What happens to my earnings if I unstake?

Whatever you earned up to that moment is still yours and is paid out as usual. Tokens that are unlocking stop earning new fees.

Can the fee or its split ever change?

Not for a token that has launched. Its fee and the split are frozen in its pool, and the staking contract accepts no token on other terms. Nobody has a key that can edit them.

Which network is this on, and what do I need?

The launchpad is on Base. You need a Base wallet with a little ETH: trades and fees are in ETH, and a transaction costs well under a cent. Funding agents also works from Solana.

Is the launchpad open?

Yes. You can launch, trade and back tokens from the Launchpad page.
